Our story

Shankala started with a simple idea: we missed the food we grew up with. The egg waffles from a street-corner stall, curry fish balls on a skewer, a cup of silky milk tea on the way home. These were everyday tastes in Hong Kong, and we wanted to share them with our new home.

We started out selling at AsiaFest in Edmonton, and the response from the community has kept us going. Everything we make is inspired by real Hong Kong recipes. Come say hi at our next event!
